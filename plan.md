## Risk Analysis Using TAME

TAME helps me think about problems that could happen in my project.

* **T — Threat:** What could go wrong?
* **A — Assumption:** What am I counting on?
* **M — Mitigation:** What can I do to help prevent the problem?
* **E — Escalation/contingency:** What will I do if the problem happens?

The probability and impact numbers go from 1 to 5. I multiply them to get the risk score.

### Risk Monitoring Plan

* Look at the risk table at the end of each week.
* Mark a risk as closed when the problem is finished or checked.
* Add a new risk if a new problem comes up.
* Pay close attention to deadlines, extra features, broken links, private information, and submission instructions.

## Quality Checklist

Before I submit, I will check that:

* [ ] The website has exactly two pages.
* [ ] Page One has my name, introduction, About Me information, skills, a visual, and a link to Page Two.
* [ ] Page Two has my work experience, education, CMPA classwork, projects or assignments, career goals, and closing section.
* [ ] The page links work.
* [ ] The design is simple and matches on both pages.
* [ ] The text is easy to read.
* [ ] I checked spelling and grammar.
* [ ] The information is correct and appropriate for my classmates and instructor.
* [ ] I did not include private or confidential work information.
* [ ] I saved a backup copy.
* [ ] I submitted the project by the deadline.

## Change Rule

Before making a change, I will check that it does not break the two-page limit, deadline, or privacy rules. I will only add extra features after I finish the required work.

## When the Project Is Done

The project is done when my two-page **Learn About Me** website meets the assignment requirements, passes the checklist, does not include private work information, and is submitted by the deadline.
