# Project Retrospective

**Project:** Hi, Get to Know Me  
**Role:** Project creator  
**Goal:** Create a webpage with information about me.  
**Main deliverable:** Help people better understand me and my current job at GM.

## Introduction

I created this webpage to introduce myself and explain what I do at General Motors. My name is Eric Atwood, and I work as an Electrical Diagnostician on the Arlington Electrical Team at Arlington ASSY. The goal was to give readers a simple understanding of who I am and what my job involves.

## What went well?

The project had a clear purpose. I focused on sharing information about myself and my work instead of trying to include too many unrelated details. Including my job title, team, and location gave the page a useful starting point.

The webpage format also worked well because it made the information easy to organize and update. I was able to connect the personal side of the project with my professional experience at GM. This made the page more meaningful than just listing basic facts about myself.

## What went wrong?

The biggest challenge was deciding how much information to include. The phrase “information about me” is broad, so it was easy to think of more details than the page really needed. I also had to balance personal information with professional information so the page would sound appropriate for a work-related audience.

The project would have been easier with a clearer outline at the beginning. Without one, I spent more time deciding what belonged on the page and how to organize it.

## What would I do differently?

If I completed the project again, I would start with three sections: who I am, what I do at GM, and what I want readers to remember. I would also identify the audience before writing so I could decide how much technical information to include about electrical diagnostics.

I would ask someone to review the page before finishing it. Their feedback could help me make sure the page sounds like me, is easy to understand, and gives a respectful picture of my work. I would also plan the content earlier and use a checklist to make sure the important details are included.

## Conclusion

Overall, the project was a good way to introduce myself and explain my current job at GM. The clear purpose and simple webpage format worked well. The main improvements would be planning earlier, deciding on the audience, and getting feedback before the final version.
