# Project 03: Final Project
## Project Scope Statement
### Project Information
* **Student:** Eric Atwood
* **Class:** CMPA
* **Project:** Project 03: Final Project
* **Project name:** Learn About Me Portfolio
* **Audience:** My instructor and classmates
* **Website size:** Two pages
* **Completion:** End of the semester
## Project Purpose
For this project, I am going to make a website about myself. The website will help
my instructor and classmates learn more about me.
I will talk about my job, my skills, my education, my projects, and my future
goals. I chose this idea because I want to show who I am and what I have learned.
## What I Plan to Make
I plan to make a simple two-page website. The website will be called **“Learn About
Me.”**
I work as an Electrical Diagnostician on the Arlington Electrical Team at Arlington
Assembly Plant. I will explain my job in a general way. I will not put private or
confidential work information on the website.
The website will have two pages:
### Page One: Home and About Me
This page will have:
* My name
* A short introduction
* Information about me
* My skills
* A picture or simple design
* A link to the second page
### Page Two: Experience and Goals
This page will have:
* My work experience
* My education
* My CMPA classwork
* Projects or assignments
* My career goals
* Contact information or a closing message
## My Goals for the Project
My goals are to:
* Make a working two-page website.
* Tell people about myself.
* Explain my job and skills.
* Include my education and classwork.
* Talk about my future goals.
* Learn more about planning a project.
* Finish the website on time.
## What Will Be Included
The website will include:
* An About Me section
* My work experience
* My skills
* My education and schoolwork
* Some projects or accomplishments
* My career goals
* Links between the two pages
* A professional picture or other visual
* A contact or closing section
I will only use information that is okay to share with my class. I will not include
company secrets, customer information, or private documents from work.
## What Will Not Be Included
I will not include:
* More than two pages
* A blog
* An online store
* Login pages
* A database
* A mobile app
* Complicated animations
* Private company information
* A paid domain or hosting service
* Social media management
I am keeping the project small so I can finish it on time.
## Project Requirements
The website must:
* Have two pages.
* Be about me.
* Have information about my skills and experience.
* Have information about my education or projects.
* Include my career goals.
* Have working links between the pages.
* Be easy to read.
* Have a simple and consistent design.
* Have correct spelling and grammar as much as possible.
* Use accurate information.
* Not include private work information.
* Be submitted by the due date.
